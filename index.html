Upkeep

A house is a hundred quiet clocks: filters every 90 days, gutters twice a year, detector batteries annually. Miss them and small neglect becomes big repair. Upkeep hears the clocks for you.

Start the clocks

Preloaded with the maintenance every home forgets. Log when you last did each task and Upkeep computes the next due date, sorts the most overdue to the top, and rolls everything into one home-health score. Every "did it" resets the clock. Everything stays in your browser.
